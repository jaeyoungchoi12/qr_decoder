How to decode a QR code from an image

  1. Drop, paste, or select an image that contains a QR code.
  2. The QR code is detected and decoded instantly, right in your browser.
  3. Copy the result or open the link with a single click.

What is an online QR code reader?

An online QR code reader lets you read the contents of a QR code from an image file instead of scanning it with a camera. QR Decoder recognizes URLs, Wi-Fi credentials, email addresses, phone numbers, contact cards (vCard), calendar events and plain text — all in your browser, so your images stay completely private.

Frequently asked questions

Is QR Decoder free to use?

Yes. It is completely free, with no account or installation required.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. All decoding happens locally in your browser, so your images never leave your device.

Which file formats are supported?

PNG, JPG, WEBP, GIF and SVG images are all supported.

Can I read a QR code from a screenshot?

Yes. Copy the screenshot and paste it with Ctrl/⌘ + V, or upload the image file directly.

Which languages are supported?

The interface is available in English, Korean, Chinese and Japanese.
